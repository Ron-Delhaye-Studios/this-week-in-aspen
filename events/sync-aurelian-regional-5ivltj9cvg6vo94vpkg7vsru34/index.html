Venue

One of Colorado's weirdest traditions: teams of four pallbearers race elaborately decorated coffins carrying an 'Emma' 195 yards down Manitou Ave. Fan zone opens 10am, parade at noon, races ~1-2pm; after-party with Denver band RADO. FREE. Source: https://manitousprings.org/emma-crawford-coffin-races/
