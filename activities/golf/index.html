Golf in the Roaring Fork Valley

Nine courses from Aspen to New Castle — mountain golf at 8,000 feet, valley tracks along the Roaring Fork, and everything between. Public tee times and private clubs, seasonal hours, and where to eat after the round.

Late-season note (Sep 27, 2026): most valley courses close in October — Aspen Golf Club's final day is Oct 31, Lakota Links runs to Nov 30. Exact 2026 open/close dates are verified per course below; always confirm with the pro shop before driving over, especially in shoulder season.

Dick Phelps design, rated the #1 public course in Colorado and a Certified Audubon Cooperative Sanctuary. About a third of tee times go to the general public — book ahead.

Glenwood Springs — 430 Iron Bridge Rd · (970) 384-0630

2026 dates not published; typically April–October, weather permitting

The Kitchen at Ironbridge — lunch Fri–Sun 11 AM–3 PM, dinner Tue–Sat 5–9 PM; explicitly welcomes non-golfers on the patio

Glenwood Springs Golf Club

Glenwood Springs — 193 Sunny Acres Rd · (970) 945-7086

2026 season
March through October, 7 AM–close daily
Green fees (2026)
Spring/fall: 9 holes $37, 18 $55 · Peak Jun–Aug: $40/$60 · twilight after 3 PM from $33
Good to know
Tee times required (book 7 days out) · free club rental for visitors showing a hotel room key
Restaurant
Greenside Grill — full menu and bar with a big valley-view patio; lunch and dinner

A 1952 Henry Hughes classic, par 35 — voted Colorado's best nine-hole public course by the Denver Post, hosting ~35,000 rounds a year.

Lakota Links

New Castle — 1000 Clubhouse Drive · (800) 613-2946

2026 season
March 15–November 30, 2026 — the valley's longest season
Hours
Course 8 AM–dark daily · golf shop 7 AM–6 PM
Green fees (2026, cart incl.)
Prime (May 21–Oct 3): 18 holes $135 AM / $99 PM · Early/late: $89/$79 · twilight from $69
Restaurant
Lakota Lookout Bar & Grill — Wed–Sat 12–7 PM, Sunday brunch 9 AM–1 PM; public welcome

Jim Engh's mountain roller-coaster, slope 149 — tee shots dropping 100+ feet, once ranked among Golf Digest's 100 Greatest Public courses. Juniors 13 & under play free with a paying adult.
