About the publication

The Golden Hour is an independent weekly field guide to what's on in Aspen and the high country — music, art, and mountain happenings. Every listing is verified against the venue's own page or a trusted local source before publication; aggregator listings alone never make the cut.

Published from Aspen, Colorado. An Ad Astra Media publication.
