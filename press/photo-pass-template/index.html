How to use it

  • Send it to the artist's publicist or management — not the venue. Most venues (Belly Up Aspen included) route press through the band's team.
  • Timing: two to three weeks before the show. Night-of requests almost never clear.
  • Attach the press page (golden-hour press page ↗) so they can verify the outlet in one click.
  • Follow up once, three to four days out, if there's no reply.
  • After approval, confirm arrival details (check-in name, photo-pit rules) the day before.

The email

Subject: Photo pass request — [BAND] at [VENUE], [DATE]

Hello [Name / press contact],

I'm writing on behalf of The Golden Hour — an independent music and culture publication based in Aspen, Colorado — to request a photo pass for [BAND] at [VENUE] on [DATE].

The coverage would run as [a photo set with review / a preview feature] on our site, alongside our weekly what's-on guide for the Aspen area. You can see the publication here: [press page URL]

Coverage: The Golden Hour.

We shoot the first three songs, no flash, per house policy — and we're happy to share selects with your team after the show.

Thank you for your consideration.

Ron Delhaye
The Golden Hour
