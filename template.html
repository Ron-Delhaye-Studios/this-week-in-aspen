Saturday, September 26 – Saturday, October 3, 2026 · Vol. 1, No. 1

A quiet week of music, art, and high-country color — a handful of happenings worth your time, chosen for the days you're here.

Saturday September 26

Aspen Saturday Market

Saturday, September 26 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM

Downtown Aspen

Local growers, bakers, and makers line the downtown streets with produce, prepared foods, and high-country crafts. Arrive before 10 for the easiest browsing and the best selection — it winds down promptly at 2.

A gentle first morning — daylight, low-barrier, and an easy taste of the town before you settle in.

Event Information: aspenchamber.org

Saturday Night Live Music at Belly Up

Saturday, September 26 Doors 8:00 PM

Belly Up Aspen21+

Belly Up's intimate room hosts touring acts most Saturday nights through the fall. (Sample listing — confirm the evening's performer on the venue calendar before making plans.) Tickets are typically released in advance and weekend shows can sell out.

For the live-music lovers — a small room with a big sound, five minutes from the Club.

Event Information: bellyupaspen.com

Sunday September 27

Fall Exhibitions at the Aspen Art Museum

Sunday, September 27 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM

Aspen Art Museum

The museum's fall exhibitions fill the Shigeru Ban–designed galleries, and general admission is free. (Sample listing — check the museum's current exhibitions page for what's on view during your stay.) The rooftop café makes an easy midday pause with a view over town.

A natural fit for art lovers — world-class contemporary work in an unhurried Sunday setting.

Event Information: aspenartmuseum.org

Monday September 28

An Evening at the Wheeler Opera House

Monday, September 28 7:30 PM

Wheeler Opera House

The Wheeler's fall calendar runs concerts, comedy, and film most weeks in this landmark 1889 opera house. (Sample listing — the venue calendar carries the confirmed program; Monday performances are less common, so verify the date.) Few rooms in the mountains carry this kind of history.

For an evening with some ceremony — the room itself is half the experience.

Event Information: wheeleroperahouse.com

Wednesday September 30

Fall Color in Snowmass

Wednesday, September 30 Daytime

Snowmass Village

Late September is peak fall color in the high country. Ride the gondola for the aspen groves at their brightest, walk the village, and take lunch on a sunlit patio. (Sample listing — lift schedules shift with the season, so confirm operating days before heading over.)

The week's scenic centerpiece — the drive and the views alone are worth the morning.

Event Information: gosnowmass.com
