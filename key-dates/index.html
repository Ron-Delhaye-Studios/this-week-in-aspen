Key Dates

The season at a glance — opening days, closing days, the festivals worth planning a trip around, and the one long closure that changes how you get here. Every date below is checked against the official source.

The big one

Plan around the airport closure

Aspen/Pitkin County Airport (ASE) closes to all flights from April 4 to November 19, 2027 while its runway is rebuilt. The town, the mountains, and every summer festival stay open — only the flights stop.

Winter 2026–27

Opening day

Aspen Mountain & Snowmass open

Thursday, November 26, 2026

The lifts start turning on Thanksgiving Day, opening the 80th anniversary season at Aspen Snowmass.

New this season

The Nell Bell lift debuts

Thursday, November 26, 2026

A new high-speed quad replaces the Bell Mountain and Little Nell chairs, riding from Gondola Plaza straight to the top of Bell Mountain.

Festival

Wintersköl — 75th annual

December 10–13, 2026

Aspen's toast to winter since 1951: snow sculptures, the Soupsköl contest, live music, and a torchlight descent with fireworks over Aspen Mountain.

Anniversary

Aspen Snowmass 80th anniversary weekend

December 11–14, 2026

Eighty years after skiing began on Aspen Mountain in 1946, the resort marks the milestone with throwback pricing and a long weekend of on-mountain programming, side by side with Wintersköl.

Opening day

Aspen Highlands & Buttermilk open

Saturday, December 12, 2026

Once Highlands and Buttermilk start spinning, all four mountains are open for the season.

Festival

Aspen Gay Ski Week — 50th anniversary

January 17–24, 2027

The longest-running gay ski week in the country turns 50, with a week of skiing and parties capped by the Downhill Costume Contest at Gondola Plaza on Friday, January 22.

Sport

X Games Aspen

January 29–31, 2027

Buttermilk hosts the opening stop of the new MoonPay X Games League, where results now count toward season-long club standings — with concerts on Friday and Saturday nights.

Spring 2027

Closing day

Buttermilk closing day

Sunday, April 4, 2027

The first of the four mountains scheduled to wrap its season, conditions permitting.

Airport

Aspen airport (ASE) closes for runway reconstruction

Sunday, April 4, 2027 11:00 PM

The airport shuts to all flights while its runway is rebuilt and stays closed until November 19 — every spring, summer, and fall trip needs a drive-in plan or a different airport.

Closing day

Aspen Highlands & Snowmass closing day

Sunday, April 11, 2027

Highlands and Snowmass are scheduled to take their final turns of the season, conditions permitting.

Closing day

Aspen Mountain closing day

Sunday, April 18, 2027

The last scheduled day of the 80th anniversary season, conditions permitting.

Summer 2027

Festival

FOOD & WINE Classic in Aspen

June 18–20, 2027

Three days of chef demonstrations and Grand Tastings. The Classic goes ahead with the airport closed, so most guests will drive in — book rooms and ground transfers early.

Looking ahead

Airport

Aspen airport (ASE) reopens

Friday, November 19, 2027 7:00 PM

Flights return to Aspen on the rebuilt runway, just ahead of the 2027–28 ski season.

Ski opening and closing dates are scheduled dates announced by Aspen Snowmass and remain conditions permitting.
