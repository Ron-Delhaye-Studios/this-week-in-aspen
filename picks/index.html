The Concierge

Top Picks

The places Aspen locals actually go — restaurants, bars, trails, shops, services. Each one is scored, sourced, and verified before it appears here. No ads, no pay-to-play, no filler.

How a pick earns its place

  1. Public reputationGoogle and Yelp ratings weighted by review volume — a 4.9 from 40 reviews does not outrank a 4.7 from 4,000.
  2. Staying powerYears in business. A decade of winters survived counts more than a season of hype.
  3. Local corroborationConcierge desks, bartenders, and longtime locals are asked what they actually recommend — off the record.
  4. On-the-ground checkHours, prices, and the experience itself are verified in person before publication.
  5. The dealbreakersTourist traps, pay-to-play placements, and anything that can't be verified are cut — loudly if necessary.
