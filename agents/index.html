Agent methodology

Read llms.txt for the source and publication rules. Only the public AURELIAN Around Aspen and Regional Events Intelligence calendars feed events. Personal and Ritz-Carlton calendars are excluded.

Validate event details against their official sources before recommending attendance. Export timestamps describe supplied data, not a guarantee of current operating conditions. FEATURED and LISTED are editorial intake bands, not numerical scores.

Interpret dates in America/Denver. Date-only end dates follow Google's exclusive-end convention. Events remain current through their Denver end day and become historical the next day. Never infer a new recurring edition, a cancellation or a price.

Event media must depict the event and carry verified provenance; missing media remains absent. History supports fresh research, not automatic republication.
